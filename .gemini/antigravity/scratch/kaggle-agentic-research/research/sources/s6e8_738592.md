### Main Post by Author (2026-08-31 23:59:39.757000) [Votes: 131]

Thank you Kaggle for a fun Playground competition. This month, I used distributed intelligence to win 1st place. Phase 1 used a single autonomous agent, phase 2 added 2 more agents and a shared knowledge database. Then phase 3 and phase 4 added a swarm of new agents. Using <a href="https://build.nvidia.com/" target="_blank">NVIDIA Inference Hub</a>, we can pick from roughly 150 different LLM models in our agent harness!

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cdeotte/Kaggle_Images/refs/heads/main/Aug-2026/agents.png" alt="">

<h1>Phase 1 - Autonomous Agent</h1>I joined this competition 1.5 weeks ago and began using a single Codex GPT 5.6 Sol autorun agent (similar to what I did in Wellbore competition <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rogii-wellbore-geology-prediction/writeups/36th-place-gpt5-6-sol-yolo" target="_blank">here</a>). I instructed the agent to do everything. It read the competition description, downloaded the data and began building a big diverse ensemble (i.e. a solid strategy in playground comp). Every time it added another 10-50 group of models, it would compute new ensemble CV score and submit to Kaggle LB by itself. After 4 days, it had built a 380 model ensemble and reached 10th place (or better, i forget) on public LB by itself!

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cdeotte/Kaggle_Images/refs/heads/main/Aug-2026/phases.png" alt="">

<h1>Phase 2 - GPT 5.6 Sol battles Fable 5</h1>I worked with Fable 5 in Kaggle's NeuroGolf competition <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/neurogolf-2026/writeups/1st-place-kaggle-agent" target="_blank">here</a> and was mind blown with its abilities. In phase 2, we onboarded Fable 5 and another GPT 5.6 Sol. We told them to perform EDA and read our previous work. We then challenged Codex GPT 5.6 Sol to compete against Claude Code Fable 5 to see who can make the best single NN model and who could make the best single XGB model. For the next week they battled it out. Mostly they would work alone. When one was lagging, we would share tips from the leading agent to the trailing agent and soon the trailing agent would take the lead! We achieved **single model** `RealMLP CV 0.97070 LB 0.97174` and **single model** `XGB CV 0.97020 LB 0.97030`, wow amazing!

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cdeotte/Kaggle_Images/refs/heads/main/Aug-2026/top1s.png" alt="">

<h1>Single Model wins 1st Place!</h1>To my amazement, GPT 5.6 Sol collaborating (i.e. competing and occasionally sharing) with Fable 5 created a single model that by itself (without ensemble) wins 1st place in Kaggle's August Playground competition. A single model has not won Kaggle's Playground competition in 18 months! (The last was February 2025 <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/playground-series-s5e2/writeups/chris-deotte-1st-place-single-model-feature-engine" target="_blank">here</a>). This is a huge achievement and confirms that frontier models are Grandmasters at data science modeling! Furthermore, we can add every single model they built to our large ensemble to supercharge its performance!

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cdeotte/Kaggle_Images/refs/heads/main/Aug-2026/single_model.png" alt="">

<h1>Phase 3 - ChatGPT Pro - Swarm Intelligence!</h1>To help GPT 5.6 Sol and Fable 5 build even better single models, we asked Fable to build a `tar.gz` packet and write a `prompt` that we (humans) can (manually) upload to ChatGPT Pro to solicit its help. (This worked well in NeuroGolf competition <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/neurogolf-2026/discussion/704942" target="_blank">here</a>). Since ChatGPT Pro can answer multiple questions in parallel, we can submit multiple packets and multiple prompts in parallel. ChatGPT Pro works for 2 hours (on each packet/prompt) and then creates a `ZIP` file for us to download with its discoveries. When GPT 5.6 Sol and Fable 5 incorporated ChatGPT Pro insights into their work, they improved their best single model CV and LB scores!

I was amazed to witness that ChatGPT Pro discovered new tabular data feature engineering ideas that I have never seen before in my 8 years of competing in tabular data competitions on Kaggle. These are techniques that apply to this competition and every future tabular data science competition. Absolutely amazing!

<h1>Phase 4 - NVIDIA Inference Hub - Swarm Intelligence!</h1>To help us improve our ensemble's CV and LB score even more, we asked Fable 5 to interview new LLMs from <a href="https://build.nvidia.com/" target="_blank">NVIDIA Inference Hub's</a> roughly 150 potential LLM and then enlist them to help us. (Using a variety of LLM worked well in MAP competition <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/map-charting-student-math-misunderstandings/writeups/18th-place-pyramid-ensemble" target="_blank">here</a>). Fable 5 assessed the skills of

<ul><li>Nemotron 3 Ultra</li><li>DeepSeek V4 Pro</li><li>Kimi K3</li><li>Gemini 3.7 Flash</li><li>Opus 5</li><li>Qwen 3.8 27B</li></ul>After assessing each LLM's data science abilities, Fable 5 assigned each LLM multiple jobs to improve our ensemble's CV score and LB score.

<h1>Conclusion - Mind Blown!</h1>In conclusion, I am completely mind blown at the ability of today's frontier LLMs. I have been a top Kaggle competitions Grandmaster for 6+ years now and I can say that the work I witnessed these agents perform in this competition exceeds what I believe is humanly possible. We are certainly in the new era of agentic data science.

### Comment #20 by Author (2026-09-02 19:16:57.393000) [Votes: 2]

These are great suggestions. I basically do everything or try to :-)

(1) yes i use all public info both in this comp and previous comps (2) yes i use my domain knowledge to help

But I also run agents without giving them this first. In the same way that the best human Kaggle team is where humans work independently first, i do this with my agents. I usally begin a new agent with "Here's the data build a strong model". And let it try from nothing. Then i give it some clues and it tries again. I repeat this for different amounts of time. Then finally i give it public knowledge and my knowledge and say to combine the new ideas with its ideas to do better. I add all of its work to the ensemble (i.e. models it built without knowledge and models it built with knowledge)

And then there are lots of hybrids to this. I then ask a smart agent (who has been working for a while) to make an introduction starter folder for another agent and only give it some info inside. So maybe it gives it some public ideas and some domain knowledge ideas. But not everything. And the new agent starts from that.

So there are lots of way to apply the no knowledge, versus limited knowledge, versus all knowledge tricks and i try them all.

### Comment #60 by Author (2026-09-02 15:56:40.040000) [Votes: 5]

My advice is going to sound circular and paradoxical. The best way to use agents is to ask agents how to use agents!

Get one agent up and running. Preferrably one of the best like GPT 5.6 or Fable 5.1. Then have the agent do some work itself (i.e. EDA, build models, etc) because just like humans, agents learn from doing. (They have a 1 million token context which is their previous conversation, thinking, and tool calling history. This gets appended to each new prompt we ask and represents their working memory). Afterward this agent is really smart.

Next, ask the agent. "Can you help me use agent XYZ? Can you create a folder of resources and give me instructions and a prompt to start agent XYZ?". That is how I incorporated using ChatGPT and the 6 models from NVIDIA Inference Hub efficiently.

Lastly, after having agents work for an N hour campaign, always have them write what they did and what they learned afterward. Every agent needs to continually write MD files logs of what it is doing and what it did. Records, records, records (as an accountant may say). These are invaluable. At one point GPT5.6 hit a plateau and couldn't improve its single model. I had Fable 5 read its logs. Then Fable 5 told me what to tell GPT5.6. After that GPT5.6 built a better single model than Fable 5! Mind blown!
